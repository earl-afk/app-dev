# My Favorite Movie: DEAD POETS SOCIETY

## About the Movie
**Dead Poets Society** is a Dead Poets Society is a 1989 coming-of-age drama about an inspiring English teacher who encourages his students to seize the day and embrace individuality.

## Main Actors
1. Robin Williams as John Keating.
2. Robert Sean Leonard as Neil Perry.
3. Ethan Hawke as Todd Anderson

## Why it was My favorite
-The powerful message about the quote "Carpe Diem."

-It was a film that invites repeated reflection
